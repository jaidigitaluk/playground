- 🚫 **NO CLICHÉ OPENERS:** Never open with *"In today's fast-paced digital world..."*, *"In the modern business landscape..."*, or *"Marketing is more important than ever."* Open immediately with the commercial stakes or revenue friction.
- 🚫 **BAN VAGUE FLUFF:** Ban words like *"synergy"*, *"holistic solutions"*, *"bespoke marketing"* (without defining specifics), *"game-changer"*, and *"seamlessly"*.
- 🚫 **NO PASSIVE VOICE:** Write in active, direct voice (*"We audit your acquisition funnel"* not *"Audits are performed on funnels"*).
- ✅ **CONCRETE EVIDENCE:** Frame every claim around measurable outcomes (*"90-day execution sprints"*, *"pipeline acceleration"*, *"CAC reduction"*).
